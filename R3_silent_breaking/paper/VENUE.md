# R3 venue decision (Claude, supervisor-delegated, 2026-11-18)

**Target: ICSME 2027, Research Track** (IEEE International Conference on Software Maintenance and Evolution, 7–11 September 2027).
**Fallback: Empirical Software Engineering (EMSE) journal.** It has no deadline; extend to a journal version if ICSME rejects.

## Why ICSME
- **Topic.** The paper studies how REST services evolve and how breaking changes reach clients. This is ICSME's core scope, and the closest prior REST-deprecation study (Yasmin et al.) appeared at ICSME 2020.
- **Size.** One mining study with four RQs fits 10 pages.
- **Open science.** ICSME evaluates replicability for empirical work. The frozen-plan and replication archive design matches this.

## Alternatives considered
- **MSR 2027.** The deadline was 23 October 2026 and has passed; source: conf.researchr.org/dates/msr-2027.
- **ICSE 2028 and FSE 2027.** These are too broad for the contribution size, and the FSE 2027 deadline has passed.
- **ISSTA 2027.** R1 already targets it, so we avoid sending both papers to the same venue.

## Format rules (from the ICSME 2026 CFP; to be re-checked when the 2027 CFP is posted)
- **Length.** "Papers must not exceed 10 pages (including figures and appendices) plus up to 2 pages that contain ONLY references."
- **Template.** IEEE two-column conference format, `\documentclass[conference]{IEEEtran}`. The source is `latex/main.tex`, generated by `latex/md2ieee.py`.
- **Review.** Double-anonymous:
  - omit author names;
  - cite self in the third person;
  - the title must differ from any arXiv preprint, and must not be used publicly during review.
- **AI disclosure.** IEEE requires disclosure of AI-generated content, naming the system and the sections it affected. The draft has a "Disclosure of AI use" section. **The authors must review and complete it.**
- **Deadlines.** ICSME 2026 had an abstract deadline of 27 February and a paper deadline of 6 March. ⚠️ ICSME 2027 is assumed to be similar, but this is not yet published; check conf.researchr.org/home/icsme-2027.

## Remaining author actions before submission
1. Compile `latex/main.tex`. This machine has only `texlive-basic`. Either use Overleaf (upload `paper/latex/` and `paper/figures/`), or run `sudo pacman -S texlive-latex texlive-latexrecommended texlive-latexextra texlive-fontsrecommended`. Then run `pdflatex main; bibtex main; pdflatex main; pdflatex main`. Check the page count (at most 10 + 2).
2. Upload the anonymised replication archive to an anonymous host (for example Zenodo or OSF with an anonymous view-only link). Put the link in the Data availability section.
3. Do the human spot-check of `client_impact` (20 items) and add the agreement to §6.
4. Complete and confirm the AI-use disclosure.
